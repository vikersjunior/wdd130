Ghana

Ghana​‍​‌‍​‍‌ is a beautiful country situated in the western part of Africa, known for its rich culture, nice people, and lively past. Because of its friendly nature and tranquil atmosphere, it is frequently referred to as the "Gateway to Africa". Ghana has a wide range of magnificent natural sceneries, from thick rain forests and waterfalls to the beaches with golden sand along the Atlantic coast. The country is also distinguished by its colourful festivals, traditional music, and tasty foods such as jollof rice and kelewele. Ghana,​‍​‌‍​‍‌ with a lively Accra as its capital city, is still a growing hub of fresh ideas, innovation, and a proud African ​‍​‌‍​‍‌heritage.
